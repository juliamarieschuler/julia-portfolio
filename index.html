01 / About

Building the conditions for better work as teams grow.

I work at the intersection of people, structures, and change. My focus is helping organizations scale without losing clarity, trust, or momentum.

Whether teams are growing from a small, close-knit group into a broader operating model, or leaders need more effective ways to align, decide, and move together, I support the work that makes change tangible.

My experience spans digital teams, agency environments, and growth-oriented organizations — all of which required thoughtful structure, strong communication, and practical change leadership.
